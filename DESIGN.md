## Brand & Style

The visual narrative draws inspiration from contemporary upscale Thai culinary arts—balancing heritage craftsmanship with sleek architectural restraint. It balances the elemental warmth of wood-fired kitchens, hand-thrown terracotta ceramics, and aged golden brass with disciplined editorial minimalism.

The interface targets discerning gourmands, culinary travelers, and high-touch hospitality patrons looking for effortless table reservations, private dining inquiries, and seasonal tasting menu explorations. 

The aesthetic marries **tactile luxury** with **editorial minimalism**:
- Generous, measured negative space evoking the unhurried cadence of fine dining.
- Warm, tactile parchment surfaces reminiscent of artisanal mulberry and handmade fibrous stationery.
- Polished, razor-thin metallic framing that evokes brass plating and patinated teakwood joinery.
- Atmosphere is warm, aromatic, poised, and immersive without visual clutter.

## Colors

The color system centers around a foundational culinary palette rooted in Thai gastronomy and architectural raw materials:

- **Primary (`#9B422B` - Roasted Clay Terracotta):** Anchors high-priority actions, interactive states, selected tasting courses, and primary reservation calls-to-action. Reflects clay pots and baked earth.
- **Secondary (`#2E1A14` - Charred Teakwood):** Provides deep, authoritative grounding for primary headings, deep-set footers, high-contrast badges, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359` - Polished Brass Gold):** Accents seasonal markers, Michelin/award distinctions, active highlights, dietary emblems, and refined borders.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Silk Parchment):** The foundation canvas, providing an organic, warm alternative to sterile whites, reducing eye strain and evoking textured linen napkins.

### Functional Palette Extensions
- **Surface Layer 1:** `#F3EFE6` (Linen card container)
- **Surface Layer 2:** `#E9E3D5` (Inset fields, sunken controls)
- **Text Primary:** `#241611` (Near-charcoal brown for optimal contrast against parchment)
- **Text Muted:** `#73625A` (Aged peppercorn for secondary metadata and dietary descriptions)
- **Success / Botanical:** `#3D5941` (Kaffir leaf green for booking confirmed states and herbal notes)

## Typography

The type system pairs **Noto Serif** with **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Noto Serif** captures the literary gravitas of classical Thai culture and high-end gastronomy. Used for tasting course titles, overarching restaurant philosophies, and hero banners. It renders bilingual scripts (Latin and Thai) with balanced weight.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces a warm, clean, geometric rhythm for body text, course ingredient breakdowns, time-slot pickers, and booking forms, ensuring legibility at smaller scales.

Use uppercase styling strictly on `label-sm` and `label-md` with tracking between `+0.04em` and `+0.08em` for badges, table categories, and currency notations. Never apply italicization to sans-serif body text; reserve italics for foreign culinary terminology (e.g., *Nam Prik*, *Gaeng Som*) rendered in Noto Serif.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet landscape, collapsing to a 6-column grid on tablet portrait and a 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (min 1200px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) margins. Max-width container capped at `1320px` to maintain comfortable eye movement.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 6 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) outer margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` (20px) outer margin.

Vertical spacing adheres to an asymmetric cadence: food imagery and tasting sequence steps utilize expansive vertical gaps (`space-xl` and beyond) to instill an unhurried, luxury feel, while form fields and time-slot reservation matrices compress down using `space-sm` and `space-md` for concise utility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering**, **warm tinted shadows**, and **whisper-thin brass hairlines** rather than standard gray dropshadows.

1. **Flat / Base Level:** The primary parchment canvas (`#F9F6F0`). All content begins grounded on this natural surface.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Cards, Menu Sections):** Constructed using `#F3EFE6` with an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.25)` (brass hairlines). Shadows are diffuse and warm: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 26, 20, 0.05)`.
3. **Floating Overlays (Reservation Drawer, Date-Time Pickers, Modal Dialogues):** Cast a deep teak shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(46, 26, 20, 0.16)`. Background scrims use `rgba(46, 26, 20, 0.45)` paired with a soft backdrop blur (`blur(4px)`).
4. **Debossed / Inset Elements (Search inputs, selected calendar cells):** Display an inset tone of `#E9E3D5` with an inner shadow: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(46, 26, 20, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometry. Corner radii are restrained and architectural:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards):** `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Larger Containers (Modal panels, Tasting Menu plates):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Reservation Badges & Micro-Pills:** Rounded capsules (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for table status pills (e.g., "Available", "Chef's Counter", "Waitlist Only") and wine-pairing tags to contrast against the architectural forms of cards.
- **Visual Dividers:** Hairline borders of `1px` with subtle 24px brass ornamental center notches for section delimiters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Reserve Table", "Confirm Booking"):** Solid Terracotta background (`#9B422B`), parchment text (`#F9F6F0`), 4px border radius. Hover transitions to `#823521` with a slight warm glow. Height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile.
- **Secondary / Pairing CTA ("Explore Wine Program"):** Transparent background, `1px solid #D4A359` (Brass), Charred Teak text (`#2E1A14`). Hover fills with `#F3EFE6` and darkens border.
- **Tertiary / Link CTA:** Charred Teak text with an underline offset of 4px in `#D4A359`.

### Dining Reservation Badges & Chips
- **Time-Slot Chips:** Interactive capsules. Default state: `#F3EFE6` with `1px solid #E9E3D5`. Hover: `1px solid #9B422B`. Selected: `#2E1A14` background with `#F9F6F0` text and an amber dot indicator.
- **Status Badges:** Small-scale uppercase tags. Gold (`#D4A359`) at 15% opacity with solid gold text for "Signature"; Green (`#3D5941`) at 15% opacity with dark green text for "Seats Open".

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Tasting Course Card:** Crafted on `#F3EFE6` parchment. Uses a subtle `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.2)` border. Top metadata features Roman numerals in Noto Serif Gold.
- **Ingredient & Allergen Rows:** Clean horizontal alignment with dot leaders separating course titles from seasonal origins, accompanied by minimal allergen indicator icons.

### Inputs & Date-Time Pickers
- **Text Fields & Dropdowns:** Baseline height of 48px, background `#F3EFE6`, border `1px solid #E9E3D5`. Focus shifts border to `#9B422B` with zero harsh box-shadow (only a 1px brass outline). Labels float above in `label-md` uppercase teakwood brown.
- **Guest Counter:** Segmented stepper control in teakwood framing, tactile plus/minus toggles with terracotta feedback on tap.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom square checkboxes (4px radius) and circular radios with a `1.5px solid #73625A` border. Checked states feature solid `#9B422B` fills with parchment white checks or center dots.
